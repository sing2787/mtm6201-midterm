How It Works

  1. 01 — Initial Consultation

    We schedule an initial consultation to discuss health history, goals and dietary needs.

  2. 02 — Assessing Your Needs

    Our team performs a detailed assessment and prepares a customized strategy.

  3. 03 — Personalized Nutrition Plan

    A tailored plan with meal timing, portions and recommendations is prepared.

  4. 04 — Meal Planning & Recipes

    We provide recipes and weekly plans aligned to your preferences.

  5. 05 — Ongoing Support

    Regular check-ins and support keep you on track.

  6. 06 — Progress Tracking

    Use our tools to log progress and refine the plan as needed.

  7. 07 — Regular Check-ins

    We review progress and adjust the plan periodically.

  8. 08 — Education & Resources

    Access articles, workshops and videos to learn more about nutrition.

  9. 09 — Fine-Tuning & Adjustments

    As needs evolve, we refine your program to match new goals.

  10. 10 — Sustainable Lifestyle Habits

    We focus on lifelong, sustainable habits for lasting change.
